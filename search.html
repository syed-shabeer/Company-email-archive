📘 How to use Email Archive

1
Find your email — Use the search bar on the left. Type any keyword from the subject, sender, or body. Filter further by folder, date, or sender.
2
Click any email card to view its full content in the right panel.
3
Copy the filename — Scroll to the bottom of the email view and click "📋 Copy Filename" to copy the .msg filename.
4
Open the Emails folder — Click "📂 Outlook Items" in the top right. This opens the Emails folder (same location as this file) directly in File Explorer.
5
Search in File Explorer — Once the Emails folder is open, paste the copied filename into the File Explorer search box (top-right) and press Enter to locate and open the .msg file in Outlook.
